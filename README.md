# Nightstand Player

A bedside music player and clock for Android. It plays your liked songs from **YouTube Music** and **Spotify** (no Spotify Premium needed), mixes in similar songs, and shows a big clock next to the music.

**Download:** get `NightstandPlayer.apk` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest). Once installed, the app checks here for updates and installs them itself.

## About

Nightstand Player is made by one person, in his spare time. He hopes you enjoy it as much as he enjoyed making it.

- Retro Amp lights follow the music, and its list shows the next 5 songs (tap one to play it)

## Features

**Music**
- Signs in to YouTube Music and Spotify inside the app and merges your liked songs into one library
- Shuffle play with similar songs mixed in (off, some or lots)
- Your YouTube Music and Spotify playlists, plus genre playlists made automatically from your likes
- Live search for songs, artists and albums, and instant search of your own songs (works offline)
- Like button that saves back to YouTube Music or Spotify
- Crossfade between songs, and next songs loaded early so they start at once
- Volume leveler, so every song plays at about the same loudness
- Offline music: on Wi-Fi it keeps about 1.5–2 hours of your likes on the phone for when there's no internet

**Clock**
- 8 clock faces (Digital, Analog, Flip, Minimal, Neon, LED, Rings, Words): swipe across the clock to switch
- 8 clock colours, and clock pictures (Beach, Night, Campfire, Forest) that recolour the app
- The clock fills the screen after 2 minutes without a touch while music plays

**Look**
- Skins: Retro Amp, Click Wheel, Turntable and Cherry Blossom
- Six colour themes: Ember, Ocean, Mint, Rose, OLED and Paper
- Night mode that slowly dims the screen at night

**Bedtime**
- Sleep timer that fades the music out
- Alarm that wakes the screen and fades the music in, on the weekdays you choose

**Everyday**
- Pauses for other apps' sound and starts again after
- Small "Offline" / "Connection unstable" bar
- In-app updates with a "what's new" list
- Report / Request button to send bugs and ideas

## Install

1. On your Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer), download `NightstandPlayer.apk` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Open it. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or file manager; allow it for this install.
3. Open **Nightstand Player**, tap the gear, and sign in to YouTube Music and/or Spotify.

Each release lists the APK's SHA-256 checksum; Settings → About → "Check app files" compares your installed app with it.

## Please note

This is an unofficial hobby app. It uses private APIs of YouTube Music and Spotify that may change and break it at any time, and using them may go against those services' terms. Your logins stay on your phone. Use it at your own risk.
